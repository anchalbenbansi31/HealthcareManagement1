HEALTHCARE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Version 2.0 – Enhanced Specification

1. Patients can register and log in securely.
2. Patients can search doctors by name, specialization, and department.
3. Patients can book, cancel, and reschedule appointments.
4. The system displays real-time doctor availability.
5. Patients receive appointment confirmations and reminders through email or SMS.
6. Patients can view appointment history.
7. Patients can pay appointment fees online.
8. Doctors can manage appointment schedules.
9. Doctors can maintain electronic medical records.
10. Doctors can create and update digital prescriptions.
11. Administrators can view a dashboard and generate reports.
12. Role-based access is provided for patients, doctors, and administrators.
13. The system maintains secure access to patient information.
