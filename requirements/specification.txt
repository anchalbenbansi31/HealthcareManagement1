HEALTHCARE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Version 1.0 – Initial Specification

1. Patients can register and log in to the system.
2. Patients can view available doctors.
3. Patients can search for doctors by name or specialization.
4. Patients can book and cancel appointments.
5. Doctors can view their appointment schedules.
6. Doctors can maintain basic patient medical records.
7. Administrators can manage doctor and patient information.
