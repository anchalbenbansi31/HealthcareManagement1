HEALTHCARE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Version 1.1 – Updated Specification

1. Patients can register and log in to the system.
2. Patients can search for doctors by name, specialization, and department.
3. Patients can book and cancel appointments.
4. Patients can reschedule appointments.
5. The system displays doctor availability and time slots.
6. Patients receive appointment confirmation notifications.
7. Patients can view their appointment history.
8. Patients can update their profile information.
9. Doctors can view their appointment schedules.
10. Doctors can maintain patient medical records.
11. Administrators can manage doctor and patient information.
